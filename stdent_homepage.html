About Our College

SDI College

SDI College is a leading educational institution committed to providing quality education and creating future leaders.

Our college provides a modern learning environment, experienced faculty members, and various extracurricular activities.

We believe that education is not only about academic knowledge but also about developing confidence, creativity and leadership skills.

SITA DEVI INTERMEDIATE COLLEGE was established in1978. It is located in Rural area. It is located in BHADOHI block of BHADOHI district of Uttar Pradesh. The school consists of Grades from 9th to 12th. The school is Co-educational. In this school academic session starts in April.
